# LV-426 hidden object

LV-426 (Acheron) is an opt-in fictional moon from the Alien setting. It is not part
of the public 31-object catalog and does not appear in normal Select all actions.
Holding the Objects title for three seconds checks it and makes it available in
the catalog. An early release or cancelled gesture does nothing. Repeating the
hold keeps it selected. Unchecking it, including through deselect-all, removes it
from the catalog and scene immediately. A saved checked state survives reopening;
no separate permanently unlocked flag exists. Filters and sorting apply normally.
The reveal scrolls to the new row when it passes the current filters. The catalog
total is 32 while the fictional moon is selected, and returns to 31 after removal.

The default catalog places the 39.3-ly scenic reference between Alpha Centauri AB
and TRAPPIST-1e. Explicit distance sorting uses the same reference immediately,
before asynchronous refresh, so a newly revealed entry is never temporarily
sorted as missing. Alphabetical and other physical orders use their usual keys;
unknown fields still remain last. A real reveal displays the existing customized
Android bottom message: “You unlocked the secret planet: LV-426!” in all 20
languages. Repeating the hold while already checked does not repeat the message;
unchecking and revealing it again does.

## Data and pointing

The details explicitly identify fictional reference values. The original lore
diameter is approximately 1,200 km; later publications give incompatible sizes.
It must not be treated as a measured diameter or used to infer mass/density.
The lore gravity is 0.86 standard Earth g, and the rotation reference is two
hours. The ALIEN RPG mean temperature of 10 °C describes the terraformed colony
era, independent of the app's chosen observation date. Calpamos is the parent.
Mass, density, atmospheric pressure, orbital period/elements and physical phase
are not supplied. Ordinary app units and uncertainty formatting remain in use.

References consulted on 2026-10-06:

- [Xenopedia: Acheron](https://avp.fandom.com/wiki/Acheron_(LV-426)):
  parent, fictional geography, diameter discrepancy, gravity and rotation.
- [AvP Central: Acheron guide](https://www.avpcentral.com/lv-426-full-guide-to-acheron-locations):
  cross-check of the original diameter, moon classification and setting.
- [ALIEN RPG publisher](https://freeleaguepublishing.com/games/alien/) and the
  licensed game's LV-426 reference entry: terraformed climate and mean temperature.
- [SIMBAD: Zeta2 Reticuli](https://simbad.cds.unistra.fr/simbad/sim-basic?Ident=Zeta2+Reticuli):
  ICRS/J2000 direction and proper motion of the real host-star reference.

Pointing and the daily path use the real Zeta² Reticuli direction and proper
motion, with an approximate 39.3 ly reference distance. This is an illustrative
placement, not a claim that LV-426 exists or that its orbit is known. No invented
moon ephemerides, local Solar System engine body or extra network provider is
introduced. The sphere's visible surface orientation and lighting are illustrative;
phase and physical observer-facing geometry remain unavailable.

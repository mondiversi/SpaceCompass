Original Mondiversi generative space score for Space Compass

Original procedural composition and synthesis, with no third-party music or samples.
The website chord/bell score is synthesized continuously using local oscillators,
envelopes, stereo panning and partitioned convolution; no recording is bundled.
Reproducible source: SpaceCompassAmbient* Kotlin files. No audio network or extra library.

Historical Quiet Orbit recordings retain their original credit and GPL-3.0 licence;
scripts/GenerateAmbientMusic.py preserves that earlier composition's development source.

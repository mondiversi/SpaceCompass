Original procedural composition and synthesis, with no third-party music or samples.
96-second stereo seamless loop; Ogg Vorbis, 44.1 kHz. Its reproducible source is
scripts/GenerateAmbientMusic.py. FFmpeg encodes the audio during development and
is not included in the Android app.

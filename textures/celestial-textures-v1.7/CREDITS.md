# External celestial graphics 1.7

Images and thumbnails only. Scientific facts and translations remain bundled in the application. Distributed as individually verified files in the stable celestial-textures-v1.6 release. No complete ZIP is produced.

Full images: WebP quality 90, method 6, normally 2048 × 1024. Earlier image bytes are retained exactly, including the original LV-426 resolution. Thumbnails: 128 × 128, WebP quality 90, method 6.

Only the required individual thumbnail or full image is downloaded. Missing photographs use a symbolic placeholder, not a fabricated observation.

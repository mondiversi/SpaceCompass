Space Compass celestial texture pack 1.2.1
20 WebP images; quality 90/100, encoding method 6.
19 images are 2048 x 1024; LV-426 retains its original 1774 x 887 pixels.
All image bytes match pack 1.2; no additional lossy recompression or upscaling.
The app downloads individual verified files, not the ZIP. Earlier release URLs are preserved.

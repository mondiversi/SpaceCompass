Assets: sun.webp, mercury.webp, venus_atmosphere.webp, mars.webp, jupiter.webp,
saturn.webp, uranus.webp, neptune.webp.
Maps based on NASA imagery with colour adjustments by the original publisher.
Space Compass caps maps at 2048x1024 and applies calculated lighting at runtime.
Public distribution copy: TanvirAhmedArnab/SolarSystem, SourceAssets/ThirdParty/
Textures/SolarSystemScope. Copyright/license of the maps remains with INOVE.

Pluto: NASA/JHUAPL/SwRI, New Horizons global colour mosaic.
https://science.nasa.gov/resource/pluto-global-color-map/
NASA maps are resampled at runtime for display. No endorsement is implied.

https://astrogeology.usgs.gov/search/map/europa_voyager_galileo_ssi_global_mosaic_500m
USGS public-domain products; original source credits retained. Native grayscale
512x256 browse mosaics, not new telescope photographs or invented colour.

Sedna diameter: Pal et al. 2012, https://arxiv.org/abs/1204.0899;

Optimized distribution: WebP quality 90 at original packaged dimensions. Europa keeps its smaller original JPEG.

Io and Europa 2048x1024 colour display derivatives:
NASA Visualization Technology Applications and Development (VTAD), original
4096x2048 albedo maps embedded in the official NASA Io and Europa 3D models.
https://science.nasa.gov/resource/io-3d-model/
https://science.nasa.gov/resource/europa-3d-model/
Io source: Io_1_3643.glb, embedded color_2016_04_28.jpg (PNG RGBA raster).
Europa source: Europa_1_3138.glb, embedded color_2016_03_10.jpg (PNG RGBA raster).
Derivatives: io_2048.webp and europa_2048.webp, WebP quality 90, 2048x1024.
The NASA display maps contain processed colour and visually filled/blended
coverage; they do not assert complete measured terrain or live surface imagery.
They supersede the earlier 512x256 USGS browse derivatives for this app build.
Old io.webp and europa.jpg distribution files remain unchanged for older apps.
North-up orientation and longitude seam match the original reference mosaics.
NASA/VTAD, NASA/JPL/USGS credits retained; no endorsement is implied.
